Info

Post-Prohibition: Research, Pedagogy, Action is an interdisciplinary research network devoted to examining the histories, institutions, and afterlives of prohibition. It brings together scholars and practitioners to explore the legal, political, environmental, racial, and ethical dimensions of drug policy in a period of rapid change.

Read the full problem statement and research questions.

Annotated Bibliography

Research, pedagogy, and action are brought together through a shared commitment to critical inquiry, public learning, and collective engagement with the legacies and futures of prohibition. The bibliography extends this inquiry across scholarship in law, gender studies, history, social theory, public health, and the arts, tracing how prohibition shapes knowledge, institutions, and everyday life.

Convenors

This network is organized by scholars and educators working across interdisciplinary fields, with a focus on building collaborative conversations around research, teaching, and civic action. Its convenors bring together different forms of expertise to support dialogue, partnership, and public engagement around the politics of prohibition and the possibilities of alternative futures.
